## Generation details

Mode: built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The generated PNG files were encoded as WebP for the website without changing their composition.

Burger prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square restaurant menu photograph, full-bleed image. Create a premium, realistic single cheeseburger on a plain charcoal-black ceramic plate: sesame bun, one seared beef patty, melted golden cheddar and a hint of pickles. Centered at a low three-quarter angle; the complete burger and plate remain within frame with breathing space. Warm controlled side lighting, true food texture, dark espresso restaurant background, fine photographic grain and gentle shallow depth of field. Restrained expensive editorial restaurant photography, no exaggerated stacks or glossy CGI. No text, logos, packaging, watermarks, hands, people, or recognizable restaurant identity. Square 1:1 composition.

Restaurant prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: vertical 3:5 restaurant atmosphere photograph replacing a branded storefront image within an existing dark luxury restaurant website. Show a believable anonymous contemporary burger-and-grill restaurant interior after dark, viewed from the entrance: intimate charcoal walls, dark timber, a warm amber-lit counter, understated brass details, neatly arranged dark leather seating and soft pendant lamps. Natural architectural photography at eye level, deep shadows with clear material texture, upscale and welcoming rather than ornate. Crisp modern camera quality, realistic proportions, gentle film grain. Portrait composition, no people, no signage, no written words, no logos, no watermarks, and no recognizable real restaurant. Nothing blue or neon; warm espresso, muted brass and charcoal palette.
